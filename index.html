Straight answers

Questions shop owners actually ask.

How long does setup actually take?

Most shops are ringing sales on their own till the same day. Add the items you sell, set a reorder point on the ones you’re always running low on, and hand each staff member their own sign-in. The whole thing reads like your counter, not a spreadsheet.

Do staff need their own accounts?

Yes, and that’s the point. A cashier signs in and rings sales; a stockist signs in and receives goods. You, the owner, see cost, margin, and the full books. Nobody shares a login, and every sale is tied to the person who made it.

What does “live stock” really mean?

It means the number on the screen moves when the till moves. Ring a sale of Ankara 6y and the stock figure for Ankara 6y drops with it, no end-of-day recount. When it passes your minimum, StockDesk Inventory raises the low-stock alert automatically.

Is there a free trial?

Start free, no card, no expiry date to remember. Ring real sales on a free shop, and upgrade when you want margin, reports, and your full staff on it.

I keep records in a receipt book today. Can I move?

Yes, at your pace. Put your current items into StockDesk Inventory as you sell them, the products you ring today become your ledger. You don’t need to type in years of old books to start; just start the till.

How do I pay for a plan?

Pay monthly in UGX with MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money. No international card required. Cash sales at your till stay cash, StockDesk only tracks what you sell; customer payment method is up to you.

Does it work when the network is slow or drops?

Yes. Ring sales on a slow connection; when the line comes back, stock and sales sync automatically. Built for real counters where data is not always perfect, not only for fibre offices.

Can I print or share a receipt?

Yes. After a sale you can print a thermal receipt or share a simple digital slip. Your shop still looks professional even when the customer only wants a quick proof of purchase.

Is the product only in English?

The app is in clear English today so any cashier can learn it fast. Support is available on WhatsApp in English and Luganda when you need a human.
