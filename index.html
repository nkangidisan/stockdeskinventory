Fast checkout

Staff roles

Low-stock alerts

Role-based access

Everyone sees the right thing. Nobody sees too much.

Every staff member signs in with their own login and gets a view matched to their job: owner, cashier, or stockist. No shared passwords, and every sale is tied to the person who made it.

Owner / Admin

The full picture: cost, margin, all branches, every staff member, and the complete books. Only the owner sees what stock cost and what the shop made on it.

Straight answers

Questions shop owners actually ask.

How long does setup take?

Most shops are ringing sales on their own till the same day. Add the items you sell, set a minimum on the ones you’re always running low on, and give each staff member their own sign-in. It reads like your counter, not a spreadsheet.

Is it free to start?

Yes. Sign up with no card, add your products, and ring real sales before you pay anything. Plans start at UGX 15,000 a month when you’re ready.

Do staff need their own accounts?

Yes, and that’s the point. A cashier signs in and rings sales; a stockist signs in and receives goods. You, the owner, see cost, margin, and the full books. Nobody shares a login, and every sale is tied to the person who made it.

What does “live stock” really mean?

It means the number on the screen moves when the till moves. Ring a sale of Ankara 6y and the stock figure for Ankara 6y drops with it, with no end-of-day recount. When it passes your minimum, StockDesk raises the low-stock alert automatically.

I keep records in a receipt book today. Can I move?

Yes, at your own pace. Add your current items to StockDesk as you sell them, and the products you ring today become your records. You don’t need to type in years of old books to start. Just start the till.

How do I pay for a plan?

Pay monthly in UGX with MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money. No international card required. Cash sales at your till stay cash: StockDesk records the sale, and how your customer pays is up to you.

Does it work when the network is slow or drops?

Yes. Ring sales on a slow connection, and when the line comes back, stock and sales sync automatically. It’s built for real counters where data isn’t always perfect, not only for offices on fibre.

Can I print or share a receipt?

Yes. After a sale you can print a thermal receipt or share a simple digital slip, so your shop looks professional even when the customer only wants quick proof of purchase.

Is the product only in English?

The app is in clear English today so any cashier can learn it fast. When you need a person, support on WhatsApp is available in English and Luganda.
