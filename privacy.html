Privacy Policy

Last updated · October 2026

Who we are

StockDesk Inventory (“StockDesk”, “we”, “us”) provides inventory and sales software for retail shops. We operate from Uganda, and the product is built in collaboration with Grittrix Technologies. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

The law we follow

We handle personal data in line with Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and its regulations. We collect personal data only for the purposes listed below, keep it accurate, protect it, and keep it only as long as we need it.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, phone number, email, and shop name when you sign up.
  • Shop data you enter: products, stock levels, sales, staff logins, and related notes.
  • Usage data: device type, browser, and basic logs that help us keep the product stable and secure.
  • Payment references for plan billing (for example Mobile Money transaction IDs). We never see your wallet PIN or full card number.

How we use data

  • To run your till, stock, and staff features.
  • To bill your plan and confirm payments.
  • To support you on WhatsApp, by email, or in the app.
  • To improve reliability, security, and product quality.

We do not sell your shop data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising.

Your staff and customer records

When you record details about your staff or customers in StockDesk, you decide what goes in and why. We process that data on your behalf, only to provide the service to you. Please make sure you have a good reason to record personal details and that the people concerned know about it.

Who can see your data

Only people you invite to your shop (the owner and the staff roles you assign) can see the data you put in StockDesk, according to their role. Our team accesses an account only to fix an issue you report or to protect the service.

Service providers

We use a small number of trusted providers to run StockDesk, such as cloud hosting and mobile money payment services (MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money). They get only the data they need to do their job and must keep it secure.

Where data is stored

Your data is stored on secured cloud infrastructure, which may include servers outside Uganda. Where data is stored outside Uganda, we use providers whose protections are at least as strong as those required under Ugandan law.

Security

We use access controls and industry-standard practices to reduce risk, but no online service is 100% risk-free. Keep staff passwords private and remove access when someone leaves the shop.

This website

This website does not use advertising cookies. We may use privacy-friendly analytics to count visits and button clicks so we know which pages help shop owners. We don’t use it to build profiles of you.

How long we keep data

We keep your shop data while your account is active. If you close the account, we delete or anonymise personal data within a reasonable period, unless the law requires us to keep certain records longer (for example billing history).

Your rights

  • Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct shop and staff details inside the app, or ask us to correct them.
  • Export your shop data, or ask us for a copy, by contacting us.
  • Object to how we use your data, or ask us to delete your account, via WhatsApp or email.

If you’re not happy with how we handled your data, you can complain to Uganda’s Personal Data Protection Office.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we’ll update the date at the top. We’ll tell account owners about important changes in the app or on WhatsApp.
